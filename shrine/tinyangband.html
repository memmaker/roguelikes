Lineage

Angband → Zangband → XAngband (Japanese) → TinyAngband. The history file says it plainly: “first version, made from XAngband 1.3.0” (in Japanese). iks took XAngband and cut it down to a game you can win in an evening. See the family tree.

Played here: TinyAngband 1.0.1 from iks3/tinyangband, English build.

Trivia

  • The official objectives, in full: “1. Go to the general store. 2. Buy a Lantern. 3. Kill Morgoth.” (lib/help/general.txt)
  • There is no autoroller: your stats are rolled to meet your class’s needs. (general.txt)
  • A Vala patron is chosen at birth; press r to reroll if you don’t like them. (general.txt)
  • The English readme still describes XAngband’s Yeek cave and wilderness, which TinyAngband removed. (readme_eng.txt)
  • 1.0.0 added 24×24 tiles and background music on Windows. (version.txt)

What's unique

  • Short: Sauron on floor 26, Morgoth on 27. A win takes hours, not months.
  • Small: 9 races, 6 classes, 156 monsters. Every one matters.
  • Random quest monsters every 5 floors.
  • Keeps Zangband’s pets, patrons and auto-pickup editor.

Code dive

  • C, about 189,000 lines in 160 files (with Japanese strings next to English ones). Autotools build.
  • Data files in lib/edit (r_info, k_info …), Hengband-style.
  • This port: WebAssembly with a canvas frontend, tiles and sound.

Manual

The full in-game help is on the site: read the manual. In the game, ? opens it.

Getting started

  1. Open the game, pick a race and class (Warrior is easiest).
  2. Go to the General Store (1) and buy a lantern.
  3. Take the stairs down and keep diving.

Help

No walkthrough exists, and the game is short enough not to need one. The help includes the Zangband newbie guide (tang.txt). Tips:

  • Quest monsters wait on floors 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.
  • Kill Sauron on 26 to open the stairs to 27.
  • After winning, retire with Q y @.

Cheats

The Zangband wizard commands remain: Ctrl+W for wizard mode, Ctrl+A for debug commands. Both mark the character as a cheater. (command.txt; not tried here)
