Lineage

Angband → Zangband → PernAngband (1998, a Zangband with dragons of Pern) → T.o.M.E. (2002). DarkGod dropped the Pern material and moved the game to Tolkien’s Middle-earth. After 2.3.5 (2008) he left to write the unrelated ToME 4, Tales of Maj’Eyal. See the family tree.

Played here: ToME 2.3.11-ah, the community branch by Bardur Arantsson on GitHub, with the Theme module included.

Trivia

  • The Pern roots: PernAngband 3.0 “changed some artifacts / unique monsters to be more pernish”. (changes.old, Nov 1998)
  • From 2.0.0 the splash screen randomly says “Tales of Middle Earth” or “Troubles of Middle Earth”. (changes.old, Jul 2002)
  • Releases had names: 1.0.0 was “Between the Darkness and the Light”, 2.0.0 “Point of No Return”, 2.1.0 “No Surrender, No Retreat”. (changes.old)
  • 2.1.0 added modules: whole games written on top of the engine. Theme, by Furiosity, is one, playable here. (changes.txt)
  • The name lives on in the unrelated Tales of Maj’Eyal (ToME 4).

What's unique

  • Skills instead of fixed classes: 58 skills; your class only sets starting points and multipliers.
  • Race, subrace and class: a Vampire Dunadan Necromancer is a valid character.
  • Gods: worship Eru, Manwë, Tulkas, Melkor or Yavanna, with piety and favours.
  • A wilderness map with towns (Bree, Gondolin, Minas Anor, Lothlórien, Khazad-dûm) and many dungeons.
  • Possession, symbiosis with monsters, mimicry, runecraft, alchemy, sentient weapons that gain levels.

Code dive

  • C, about 214,000 lines in 125 source files, plus Lua (tolua bindings) for spells and quests. Built with CMake.
  • Data in text files (lib/edit/*_info.txt), levels as .map files, a whole second game in lib/mods/theme.
  • This port: compiled to WebAssembly with a canvas frontend, tiles, sound and music, plus auto-explore (X), an Enter command menu and an inventory cursor.

Manual

The full in-game help (races, classes, magic schools, gods, spoilers, FAQ) is on the site: read the manual. In the game, ? opens it.

Getting started

  1. Open the game and pick a (ToME).
  2. Make a character: a Human or Dunadan Warrior is simplest; Priest or Mage are harder.
  3. Wear your gear (w), read the Adventurer’s Guide, buy potions of Cure Light Wounds and a Scroll of Word of Recall in Bree.
  4. Your first quest is in Bree; your goal is Dol Guldur. Ctrl+Q shows the quest log.
  5. Leave town to the wilderness and enter the Barrow-downs or Mirkwood.

Help

No full walkthrough exists. The help has a spoiler-free FAQ, a dungeon list and a newbie guide (? → newbie). Tips:

  • Put skill points into your weapon mastery and Combat first.
  • Use Enter for the command menu if you forget a key.
  • Dungeons have a recommended level; the list is in the help under Dungeons.

Cheats

Debug mode is built in: press Ctrl+A and answer y. The game is then not scored. Useful keys: W wish, x XP boost, c create object, j jump to level, s/n summon monster, m map. All listed in the help (Debug). Used here for the fight screenshot.
