MAG PC-1.1 · in-game help

The game's own help screens, HELP\HELP.1–HELP.7 from the original 1988 DOS release (SourceForge, Original_Dos_Version_from_1988.zip), converted from CP437. F1 shows screens 1–4 (and 5 in wizard mode); F2 then Return shows 6 and 7, where the game draws the symbols and monster letters into the blank columns: they are filled in here as the game shows them (without colour). The port's additions (Z, </> walking, the Enter menu) are on the game's help page. The instruction guide: README.DOC. © 1986, 87, 88 Michael J. Teixeira, licence.
