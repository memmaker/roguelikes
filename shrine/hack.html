Lineage

Hack is new code inspired by Rogue. Jay Fenlason wrote it in 1982 at Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School with Kenny Woodland, Mike Thome and Jonathan Payne; his READ_ME calls it “an implementation of Rogue” with 52+ instead of 26 monster types. He learned Rogue at the SFSU Logo Workshop.

Andries Brouwer took it over at the Mathematisch Centrum in Amsterdam and rewrote almost all of it; by his own note the result was “more than thrice as large”, with the dog, the long worms and the shops. His versions went out on Usenet from 1984; 1.0.3 (July 1985) was the last, and the one BSD Unix shipped. Don Kneller ported it to MS-DOS (PC Hack), and NetHack (1987) grew out of Hack. This page's game is restoHack, a restoration of 1.0.3 for modern compilers. See the family tree.

Trivia

  • Fenlason wrote it with three friends while they were students at Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School. (Wikipedia)
  • His READ_ME calls it “my first semester programming project” and gives his home phone number, adding that he has a modem and a teen-age sister. (Original_READ_ME)
  • Brouwer's READ_ME points players who want “a real cave instead of dull rectangles” to his other game, Quest. (READ_ME)
  • 1.0.3 went out on 23 July 1985 as a patch against 1.0.2, and it is the first version with a wizard (debug) mode. (NetHackWiki)
  • It has exactly one artifact: Orcrist, a two-handed sword that does extra damage to orcs. (NetHackWiki)
  • Hack still ships with many Unix systems (Debian, Ubuntu, the BSDs, Fedora), and there is even a Game Boy Advance port. (Wikipedia)

What's unique

  • A pet. You start with a little dog that fights for you, can be fed and trained, and won't step on cursed items.
  • Shops and shopkeepers. Pick up an item, pay for it, or be chased down for stealing it.
  • Things react to each other. Cockatrices stone you slowly, a dead lizard cures it; nymphs steal and teleport away; rust monsters rust your armour; floating eyes freeze you if you hit them.
  • Levels are kept. Unlike Rogue, a level you leave is stored in a file and is waiting for you when you come back, with what you dropped. Bones files let you find the ghost of an earlier player.
  • Six kinds of hero instead of Rogue's one: Tourist, Speleologist, Fighter, Knight, Cave-man (or Cave-woman), Wizard.

Code dive

  • Language: K&R C, about 19,200 lines in 77 files in the 1.0.3 source (restoHack: about 23,300 lines in 79 files after the modernisation). Files are named by topic: hack.shk.c is the shopkeeper, hack.dog.c the pet, hack.worm.c the long worm.
  • Environment: 4.xBSD and System V Unix, played on terminals through termcap escape codes, not curses. The data tables: def.objects.h lists every item, hack.monst.c every monster.
  • Saving: the game writes the current level and every visited level to files, then exits. restoHack added a versioned save header so pointers are not written raw.
  • This port: the game is not changed for the screen. Its escape codes run through a small VT100 emulator that fills an 80×24 buffer, and the map part of that buffer is drawn as tiles, in X11 and in the browser (WebAssembly via Emscripten). We fixed two bugs on the way: item labels changed after a restore, and item names showed as “<corrupted>” in WebAssembly. Source: memmaker/hack.

Getting started

  1. Open the game, type a name and answer y to “experienced player?” to pick a class (Knight or Cave-man is easiest), or n to get a random one.
  2. Move with hjklyubn or the arrow keys; walk into a monster to attack. x explores on its own.
  3. Press Enter for a menu of every command, i for the inventory.
  4. Stuck in a dead end? Search with s (or 20s). Press > to walk to the down stairs and take them.
  5. Eat when you are “Hungry”. The full key list is in the in-game guide.

Help

We found no walkthrough: every dungeon is different. Rules of thumb:

  • Never hit a floating eye in melee. Never eat a cockatrice corpse; keep a dead lizard.
  • Keep the dog close on the first levels, and let it walk over shop goods and items: it won't step on cursed ones.
  • Fight in corridors, rest back to full hit points before going down.
  • Read unknown scrolls when nothing is near; one of them may be enchant armour.
  • More: NetHackWiki on Hack 1.0.3 and RogueBasin.

Cheats

  • Wizard mode (hack -D, for the user named in config.h) exists in the source but can't be reached in the browser: the page doesn't pass -D.
  • Save scumming works in the browser: Export save before something risky, Import save if it goes wrong.
