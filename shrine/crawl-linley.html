Lineage

Linley Henzell, then at the University of Adelaide, started Crawl in 1995 as new code, not a fork; its quick-start guide calls it “one of the descendants of Rogue”. The goal, the Orb of Zot, comes from Wizard's Castle, a BASIC game. From version 3.40 (2000) the Crawl DevTeam carried on; 4.0 beta 26 (2003) was their last release. In 2006 Darshan Shaligram and Erik Piper restarted development as Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup, still developed today. See the family tree.

Played here: 4.00b26 with Mitsuhiro Itakura's tile version e070 (2005), RLTiles graphics, compiled to WebAssembly.

Trivia

  • The Orb of Zot was taken from Wizard's Castle, a BASIC text adventure. (crawl.txt)
  • You cannot call your character “bones”: “That's a silly name!” Its save files would collide with the ghost (bones) files. (newgame.cc)
  • Version 3.40 removed three “silly” monsters: the dorgi, the sword and the guardian robot. (versions.txt)
  • Henzell lost most of the source code of the early versions. (crawl.txt)
  • The tile version comes from Japan and has the full game translated: the monster table carries Japanese names next to the English ones. (mon-data.h)
  • Stone Soup, its successor, was voted Roguelike of the Year 2008 by ASCII Dreams readers. (Wikipedia)

What's unique

  • Skills instead of classes: you gain skills by using them; the class only sets the start.
  • Gods with their own conduct rules and gifts, e.g. Trog hates magic, Xom is random.
  • Branches: Orc Mines, Elven Halls, Lair, Swamp, Hive, Vaults, Hells, the Abyss and Pandemonium; runes open the Realm of Zot.
  • Many unusual species: Nagas, Centaurs, Mummies, Ghouls, Kenku, Demigods, nine colours of Draconian.

Code dive

  • C++, written first for DOS (DJGPP), then Unix curses and Windows.
  • Monster and spell data are big C tables (mon-data.h, mon-spll.h); a WIZARD build flag adds debug commands.
  • Itakura's tile version adds an X11/Windows window layer (winclass.cc, libtile.cc) and paper-doll player graphics.
  • This port: Emscripten, the window layer redirected to the page. Source: memmaker/crawl-linley.

Getting started

  1. Open the game, type a name, pick a species and class. The guide recommends a dwarf, orc, ogre or troll Fighter.
  2. Move with the arrows or hjklyubn; walk into monsters to attack.
  3. Ctrl+O explores; > walks to the stairs down.
  4. Enter opens all commands; ? is help.

Help

No walkthrough for this version. The quick-start guide covers the first games. Tips:

  • Pray (p) at an altar to join a god early; Trog and Okawaru suit fighters.
  • Train few skills, not all of them.
  • Rest to full HP before exploring further.
  • Much advice for Stone Soup still applies: CrawlWiki.
