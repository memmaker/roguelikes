Lineage

Moria → Umoria → Angband 2.x → Angband 3.0 → NPPAngband. Jeff Greene released 0.1.0 on 24 April 2003 as Angband 3.0.3 with patches (rod and wand stacking, screen dumps, auto-squelch, notes, new pits and vaults) and deliberately almost no changes to the edit files. From 0.3.0 it took in Leon Marrick’s Sangband/Demoband “4GAI” monster AI, later EyAngband-style quests, UnAngband’s terrain system (0.5.0) and a Druid class. 0.5.1 (2011, this build) moved the code base from Angband 3.0.6 to 3.1.2v2. Quickband is built on NPPAngband.

Development went on after 0.5.1: 0.5.x, then 6.x and 7.x, and a Qt version (last release 8.0.5, May 2016, per RogueBasin).

See the family tree. Sources: AUTHORS, lib/file/news.txt, the changelog, RogueBasin’s No Pet Peeves Angband (released Apr 24 2003, 0.1.0), the 0.2.0 announcement (September 2003, “based on Angband 3.0.3”), nppangband/NPPAngband (tag v0.5.1, 20 March 2011).

Trivia

  • The name: RogueBasin files it as “No Pet Peeves Angband”; the game’s own files only ever say NPP. (RogueBasin)
  • Greene announced it as a look at what Angband might have become had it “evolved creatively over the last 10 years”. (0.2.0 announcement)
  • 0.4.0 was called “Ghost on the Road” and added player ghosts; lib/bone/ holds 20 permanent ghost templates of Angband and NPP people, from Robert Alan Koeneke and Ben Harrison to Jeff Greene himself. (announcement, changelog, lib/bone)
  • Brother Steadfast, the first winner of NPP 0.3.x (Anssi Ramela’s), became a temple shopkeeper. (changelog)
  • Azathoth, Master of Darkness (level 64), changed in 0.5.0 from a demon U into a jelly J. (changelog)
  • The first version on purpose played almost exactly like Angband 3.0.3; the ogre pits were left “a little bland” for the next one. (changelog)

What's unique

  • Terrain that fights back: UnAngband’s feature system with 198 terrain kinds: lava, deep water, mud, ice, forests, burning oil, clouds that linger, runes, and monsters (and races) native to them.
  • Quests: the Adventurer’s Guild in town hands out monster, pit/nest, level, vault and themed-level quests for gold, fame and tailored rewards.
  • Themed levels (27 themes: dragons by breath, demons, undead, animals, “creeping coins” …) and wilderness and stronghold levels.
  • New classes: the Brigand (steals with P, sets monster traps with O, sling master) and the Druid (nature realm, weakest fighter); Rangers cast from druid books.
  • Smarter monsters: Sangband’s 4GAI (ranges, learning, evasion, arc breaths), true mimics, player ghosts, randarts, rod/wand stacking and auto-squelch.

Code dive

  • Language: C, 203,333 lines in 128 .c/.h files in src/ (tag v0.5.1), Angband 3.1 structure: game-cmd.c, ui-menu.c, cmd-obj.c; spells as code in x-spell.c, the huge generate.c for terrain and themed levels.
  • Data: text files in lib/edit/ (monster.txt, terrain.txt, quest.txt, limits.txt…) parsed at start; player ghost templates in lib/bone/.
  • Frontends: Windows, Mac Carbon, X11, SDL, curses, RISC OS and a Nintendo DS port (nds/).
  • History: upstream git on GitHub (tags v0.5.1 … v7.1.0); the changelog NPPchanges.txt goes back to 0.1.0.
  • This port: WebAssembly build with a canvas z-term frontend, Shockbolt tiles, sub-windows, sound and music, auto-explore (H), an Enter command menu and item menus. Source: memmaker/nppangband (our changes).

Getting started

  1. Open the game and make a character: a Dwarf or Half-Troll Warrior is forgiving; a Brigand is the stealthy option.
  2. Your torch is already wielded; wear the rest of your kit and buy Cure Light Wounds potions and Phase Door scrolls.
  3. Visit the Adventurer’s Guild once you have gone a bit deeper: quests pay in gold and fame.
  4. Go down: > walks to known stairs, H explores for you, Enter lists every command, i and e open item menus.
  5. Read the game’s help page for keys and saving.

Help

We found no walkthrough: the dungeon is random. The goal is to kill Sauron at 4950 ft and Morgoth at 5000 ft. Rules of thumb:

  • Terrain matters: don’t fight in deep water or next to lava you are not native to; burning a forest is a weapon.
  • Always carry an escape (Phase Door, Teleportation) and Word of Recall; take quests only a few levels below your comfort depth.
  • Read the tutorial and quests in the manual.
  • More: RogueBasin’s NPPAngband page, the NPPAngband ladder and the angband.live forums.

Cheats

All in this build; each marks the character, and marked characters get no high score.

  • Wizard mode Ctrl+W: asks for confirmation first.
  • Debug mode Ctrl+A, then a key: create objects, summon monsters, jump levels, map the level (tested: Ctrl+A z).
  • Cheat options (=): peek into object and monster creation, know all lore, and “cheat death” (cheat_live), which counts your deaths in your age.
  • Save scumming: Export save / Import save on the game page.
