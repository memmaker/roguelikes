Lineage

Angband → Sil (2012, last update 1.3 in 2016) → Sil-Q. Sil kept Angband’s engine and threw out most of its rules. Sil-Q “continues the development of Sil which hasn’t been updated for a while”. See the family tree.

Trivia

  • Sil drops “many generic fantasy tropes”: no wizards, no priests, no plate mail, no magic scrolls. (RogueBasin)
  • You gain experience by seeing new monsters and items and by first kills, not by grinding.
  • Magic is song: the Song of Elbereth, the Song of Staunching, the Song of the Trees. (ability.txt)
  • You don’t have to kill Morgoth. Most winners take a Silmaril while he sleeps and flee to the surface.
  • Sil-Q aims to be friendlier to newcomers and harder for veterans, and adds tiles. (RogueBasin)

What's unique

  • No classes, no spells lists: 84 abilities in 8 skills: Melee, Archery, Evasion, Stealth, Perception, Will, Smithing, Song.
  • Stealth matters: most monsters are asleep; the game rewards sneaking past them.
  • Only 20 levels (1,000 ft), and you cannot go back up to rest.
  • Smithing: forge your own weapons, armour and artefacts.
  • First Age Tolkien: Noldor, Sindar, Naugrim (Dwarves) and Edain; houses of Fëanor, Fingolfin, Hador, Bëor.

Code dive

  • C, about 142,000 lines in 53 files, on Angband’s engine. Data files in lib/edit (old Mac line endings).
  • Numbers are small and exact: attacks are rolled as d20 plus score against evasion, damage as dice against protection dice.
  • This port: WebAssembly with a canvas frontend, tiles, sound and music, plus auto-explore (P) and stair walking.

Getting started

  1. Open the game. A Naugrim (Dwarf) or Edain character with Melee is a good first pick.
  2. Spend experience on skills on the character sheet (@) and on abilities (Tab).
  3. Move quietly, keep going down, avoid fights you don’t need.
  4. At 1,000 ft, take a Silmaril from Morgoth’s crown and escape upward.

Help

No walkthrough exists. The manual covers strategy. Tips:

  • Fight in corridors; being surrounded is deadly.
  • Don’t fight everything: experience comes from seeing and discovering.
  • Songs cost voice; Elbereth scares most enemies.
