Lineage

uLarn is a direct descendant of Larn, Noah Morgan’s 1986 game. In 1987 Phil Cordier, at UC Santa Cruz, modified the Larn 12 source into Ultra-Larn, “Ularn” for short, and posted it to the Usenet group comp.sources.games; his README of 12 June 1992 calls that version “much improved over the version that was posted”. It is a sibling of Kevin Routley’s Larn 12.1–12.4 line, not a child of it.

Josh Brandt picked the code up in 2001 (“Ularn 1.5ish, patchlevel 4”), made it compile on modern Unixes and fixed many bugs; Josh Bressers moved it to git in 2006 and tagged it 1.7.0. This port builds ularn/ularn at ef42184. See the family tree, under Larn. Sources: Wikipedia, RogueBasin, the upstream README and data/Uhelp.

Trivia

  • The game calls itself “The Addiction of Ularn”. (Libregamewiki; source: main.c)
  • Instead of bats, the upper levels are infested with lemmings, which multiply. (The Larn Blog)
  • Prices exploded: one book sells for over 100,000 gold and a chest from the first volcano level for 300,000; in Larn a book fetches about 9,000. (The Larn Blog)
  • The Rambo class starts with every stat at 3, one hit point and a lance of death. (The Larn Blog)
  • A PalmOS port called iLarn exists. (RogueBasin)
  • larn.org plays Ularn in the browser too, with DOS or Amiga graphics. (larn.org)

What's unique

  • Character classes: eight of them, where Larn has none.
  • A deeper world: 15 dungeon levels and 5 volcano levels, against Larn’s 10 and 3, with demon lords from level 11 down.
  • The lance is no longer enough: it still kills anything, except demons, which it merely tickles; you need Slayer.
  • Dealer McDope’s pad sells drugs with odd effects, and the Vorpal Blade may behead.
  • Two scoreboards, one for winners, one for the dead, and your inventory is kept with your death.

Code dive

  • Language: ANSI C, about 16,300 lines in 32 files under src/. Monsters, objects and spells are tables in data.c; the shops, school and class choice live in store.c.
  • Environment: late-1980s Unix terminals via termcap, with its own output buffer (io.c); the 2001 version builds with autoconf on Linux, the BSDs, HP-UX, Tru64 and Cygwin.
  • Data files: hand-drawn maze levels (Umaps), fortune cookies (Ufortune), the help pages (Uhelp) and the scoreboard (Uscore).
  • This port: a terminal shim with side panes drawing Amiga Larn tiles, compiled to WebAssembly. Source: memmaker/ularn, upstream ularn/ularn.

Manual

uLarn has no separate manual. Its in-game help (?, from data/Uhelp) and the upstream README are here: manual. Also: Phil Cordier’s spoiler guide (README.spoilers), the changelog (2001 patchlevels plus the TODO list) and the licence (GNU GPL 2).

Getting started

  1. Open the game, type a name and pick a class; the Elf or the Dwarf make an easier start than the Rambo.
  2. You start in town. Step onto a building and answer g to go inside: the school raises stats, the DND store sells gear, the bank keeps your gold. Escape leaves.
  3. Step onto the dungeon entrance and press g to go down. Move with hjklyubn, or let ~ explore for you.
  4. c casts (D lists your spells), i shows the inventory, the Enter menu lists every command.
  5. Read the game’s help page for the rest.

Help

We found no walkthrough for uLarn. The goal: bring the potion of cure dianthroritis home from the bottom of the volcano before the time runs out. Rules of thumb, from Cordier’s spoilers:

  • The lance of death does not kill demons, and there are many more of them than in Larn: find or buy Slayer before going deep.
  • Levels 11 to 15 hold one to five demon lords each; the volcano is worse still.
  • Sell books and chests from deep levels: they are worth a fortune here.
  • More: the spoiler guide, the Ularn Spoilers 2020 of larn.org, the Larn Wiki strategies and RogueBasin.

Cheats

  • Wizard mode: = asks for a password that is compiled in (data.c), then unlocks item creation, teleport to any level and more. Not in this build: the password is read straight from standard input, which the web page has no way to feed.
  • Save scumming: Export save / Import save on the game page.
