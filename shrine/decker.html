Lineage

Decker is not descended from Rogue. It borrows the decker of the Shadowrun tabletop game: a hacker who plugs a cyberdeck into the Matrix and fights ICE to steal data. The randomly built systems, permanent death (Ironman mode) and turn-based node crawling make it a roguelike cousin. See the family tree.

Played here: Decker 1.12, ported to SDL2 and WebAssembly with a small MFC shim.

Trivia

  • Target corporations include Bubba’s Discount Bait & Tackle, PETA’s Flaky Granolas, Black Mesa Research Facilities and Spacely’s Sprockets. (game data)
  • Planned but never built: a Trojan Horse ICE that looks like a valuable file, a Time Bomb program, and a “Garbage” state for crashed programs. (To-Do.txt)
  • The source ships with an UserSubmittedGraphics folder of player art. (SourceForge)
  • The author: “I wrote it for myself, so it may be a little confusing.” (source ReadMe)

What's unique

  • Cyberpunk hacking instead of a dungeon: every level is a corporate computer system of nodes.
  • Contracts: jobs with deadlines and pay; money buys hardware and software between runs.
  • Alarms: ICE raises yellow and red alerts; stealth programs (Hide, Deceive, Smoke) matter as much as Zap.
  • Projects: write your own programs and chips over days of game time.

Code dive

  • C++ with Microsoft Foundation Classes (Visual C++ 6), about 27,000 lines in 97 files. Dialogs, bitmaps and help are Windows resources.
  • Source released under the GPL on SourceForge.
  • This port: a small MFC shim plus an SDL2 frontend, built to WebAssembly. Source: memmaker/decker.

Getting started

  1. Open the game, click New, name your decker, pick a starting bonus.
  2. View Contracts, take an easy one, then Enter the Matrix.
  3. Move node to node with the arrows; run programs from the list on the left.
  4. Finish the goal, leave through the portal, collect your pay, upgrade.

Help

No walkthrough exists; systems are random. The help file explains every program and ICE. Tips:

  • Load Hide or Deceive before moving; fighting raises the alarm.
  • Scan and Evaluate datastores; paydata sells.
  • Leave at a red alert: trace ICE ends runs.
  • Rest between runs to heal.
