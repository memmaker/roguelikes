Lineage

Angband 4.2 → Tactical Angband. A direct fork of Vanilla: same monsters, items and classes, but the game is cut in half and the escape buttons removed. See the family tree.

Played here: 0.9beta2 from tomm/tactical-angband.

Trivia

  • The first Tactical commit: “Rename Angband to TacticalAngband. Here we go”. (git history, 4 May 2022)
  • Levels are numbered 2, 4, 6 … 100, so monster and item depths still match Vanilla. (README.md)
  • The Black Market always stocks Acquirement scrolls. (README.md)
  • Monster spells and breaths do half damage, except Morgoth’s and Sauron’s. (README.md)
  • The Paladin’s Healing spell heals 300 HP, not 2000. (README.md)

What's unique

  • 50 small levels, each a quarter the size of Vanilla’s; sight limited to 10 squares.
  • One blow times “might” instead of many blows per round, for players and monsters.
  • No teleport: Teleport Self, Other and Level are gone. Instead: potions and boots of running, wands of wind and swap places, the scroll of locking.
  • No banishment; weaker summons; short, non-stacking blindness, confusion and paralysis.

Code dive

  • C, about 192,000 lines in 165 files: Angband 4.2’s modern code base. Almost all game data is text in lib/gamedata.
  • Docs are Sphinx/reStructuredText (docs/), inherited from 4.2.
  • This port: WebAssembly with a canvas frontend, tiles, sound and music.

Getting started

  1. Open the game. A Dwarf or Half-Troll Warrior or Blackguard is simplest.
  2. Buy Cure Light Wounds potions and Phase Door (rarer now) in town.
  3. Dive. There are only 50 levels; Sauron and Morgoth wait at the bottom.

Help

No walkthrough for this variant. The Angband 4.2 guides mostly apply. Tips:

  • You can’t teleport away: plan your exits, carry running potions.
  • Scrolls of locking close doors behind you.
  • Acquirement is always in the Black Market — save gold for it.
