Lineage

Angband → NPPAngband → Quickband. 1.x was built on an older NPPAngband; 2.0 was rebased on NPPAngband 0.5.1 (itself from Angband 3.1). See the family tree.

Trivia

  • Antoine’s pitch: the game takes “just a few hours to complete”. (rgra, 2005)
  • 2.0 “dealt with all incidences of Sauron, Morgoth, One Ring, Grond and Iron Crown in code”: the final bosses are Lurtz and Saruman. (QuickChanges.txt)
  • Stay at high level with lots of artifacts and the game gives you “prods to hurry up”. (QuickChanges.txt, 2.01)
  • Low Charisma makes enchanting unreliable, and high Dexterity gives speed. (QuickChanges.txt)
  • 2.0.5 introduced “the notorious wand of wall building”. (QuickChanges.txt)
  • A reviewer: “if I ever get the urge to play Angband, I will play Quickband instead”. (Do While Compiling, 2012)

What's unique

  • 12 levels: Lurtz waits on level 11, Saruman on 12. Kill Saruman to win.
  • Everything is compressed: fast levelling, good items early, more monsters per level, quick level feelings.
  • Racial knowledge: Elves identify bows by feel, Dwarves armour, Hobbits slings, Gnomes wands.
  • New classes (Barbarian, Berserker, Sage) and races (Maia, Sprite).

Code dive

  • C, about 191,000 lines in 83 files, NPPAngband’s code base (Angband 3.1 era).
  • Data in text files in lib/edit: monster.txt, object.txt, quest.txt …
  • This port: WebAssembly with a canvas frontend, UT32 tiles, sound and town music, auto-explore (H), an Enter command menu and an inventory cursor.

Manual

The in-game help (NPPAngband’s, with a tutorial) is on the site: read the manual. The Quickband changes are listed in QuickChanges.txt; ? opens the help in the game.

Getting started

  1. Open the game; a Dwarf Warrior or Half-Troll Berserker is forgiving.
  2. Buy Cure Light Wounds potions and Phase Door scrolls in town.
  3. Go down. Levels come quickly, so does danger.

Help

No walkthrough exists. The help has a tutorial (tutorial.txt). Tips:

  • Stair-scumming is limited; fight the level you are on.
  • Get resistances before Lurtz (11) and Saruman (12).
  • Dragons breathe often and have more HP than in Angband.

Cheats

NPPAngband’s debug mode: Ctrl+A, confirm, then a debug key (wish, jump level, create item …). The character is marked as a cheater. (cmd0.c; not tried here)
